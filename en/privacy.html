Privacy Policy

01Introduction and scope

This policy is issued by the owner of the ConMarn project ('we') and applies to the ConMarn Android application and its official website (together, 'the Program').

It describes what data the Program processes, the purposes of that processing, where the data is stored, under what conditions it is disclosed, and how you can exercise your rights. It does not apply to third-party services you reach through the Program; those services are governed by their own privacy policies.

02Definitions

  • Program: the ConMarn Android application and its official website.
  • Device: the Android handset on which the Program is installed and run.
  • Paired computer: a personal computer on the same local network that has established a trust relationship with the Program through the pairing process.
  • External service: any network endpoint other than the Device and a Paired computer, including endpoints you configure yourself.
  • Master switch: the single setting that governs all outbound network access, off by default.
  • Personal data: any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, recorded in electronic or other form.

03Controller and contact

The Program is developed and maintained by an individual developer. No corporate entity has been established. For the purposes of this policy, the controller is the owner of the ConMarn project.

Contact: GitHub Issues. We normally respond within fifteen working days.

04What we do not collect

To avoid misunderstanding, the Program does none of the following:

  • It does not require registration or sign-in, and does not ask for a phone number, email address or any other identity information. There is no account system.
  • It does not collect device identifiers (IMEI, Android ID, OAID and the like), MAC addresses, contacts, location, call logs or message contents.
  • It bundles no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or social sharing components.
  • It builds no server-side profile or behavioural record.
  • It does not sell, rent or otherwise provide data to any third party for marketing purposes.

06Purposes and legal bases

We process the data above only to:

  • provide the core features: conversation, voice interaction, task execution and device operation;
  • protect the security of the system and the user, including pairing authentication, per-instruction verification and risk-tiered confirmation;
  • comply with legal obligations or respond to a lawful request from a competent authority.

Processing rests on one of the following bases: your consent (for example granting a system permission or enabling the master switch); necessity for a service you have requested; and our legitimate interest in keeping the system and its users secure.

07Storage location and retention

  • Location: model files, assets, the memory store, task records, skill definitions and runtime logs all live in the app's private directory inside the Android sandbox, which other apps cannot read.
  • Retention: the memory store, task records and skills are kept until you delete them; runtime logs and temporary data are removed once they are no longer needed.
  • Deletion: uninstalling the Program deletes all of the above. The Program offers no cloud backup and no cross-device sync.

08Disclosure and cross-border transfer

All outbound network access is governed by the master switch, which is off by default.

When the master switch is on:

  • Only text is sent. Screenshots, screen images, camera frames, audio and video are never included in a request.
  • Requests go to an endpoint and API key that you configure. That endpoint may be located outside your jurisdiction, so text you enter in a conversation may be transferred abroad. The endpoint operator is responsible for its own handling of that data, and we cannot control it.
  • The built-in weather lookup is provided by open-meteo and sends only coordinates or a city name; the built-in news and quotation sources can be turned off in settings.

Turning the master switch off stops all of the above, and features that depend on external services become unavailable. We do not sell or share your data with any third party for commercial purposes.

11Security measures

  • The channel to a paired computer is encrypted with a key agreed at pairing time, and every instruction is verified individually.
  • Pairing keys and credentials are encrypted at the system level before being written to device storage.
  • Operations involving deletion, payment or authentication are subject to risk-tiered confirmation.
  • Credentials for external services are held by you; the Program never uploads them to any server.

No technical measure can guarantee absolute security. Please keep your device and its unlock credentials safe.

12Your rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access and export: view the memory store, task records and runtime logs inside the app.
  • Rectify and erase: delete a single memory item, a single task, or all data from within the app.
  • Withdraw consent: revoke granted permissions in system settings, or turn the master switch off, at any time.
  • Erase everything: uninstalling the Program deletes all data stored on the Device.
  • Object and complain: raise a concern about how this policy is applied using the contact details above.

Exercising these rights is free of charge. Where the law of your jurisdiction grants you further rights, such as access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability or objection, we will cooperate to the extent the law requires.

13Minors

The Program is not offered for use by minors under 14 on their own. If you are the guardian of a minor and believe they have used the Program without your consent, please contact us using the details above.

14Third-party links

The Program and its official website may contain links to third-party sites or services. Those sites are not covered by this policy, and we are not responsible for their content or their handling of data. We recommend reading their privacy policies before visiting them.

15Changes to this policy

Continued use of the Program after a revision takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
